Fix the two light-theme defect families, and correct the bench that hid them

The `light` theme had 40 elements below 3:1 and `v6-light` 11. The root cause
is deeper than the second `:root`: `#v9-palette` defines its palette twice —
on `:root` AND on `.tpai-shell`. A token set on the shell shadows the inherited
value for all its descendants, so the more specific `html[data-theme="light"]`
override never reaches them. Specificity is powerless against a token set lower
in the tree. Measured on the shell in `light`: --text #e6edf7 (should be
#2d4a6b), --bg-card #121826, --hull #121826, --cyan #4f8ef7. The sidebar, the
top bar, the accordion and the conversation bubbles were all running the DARK
palette inside a LIGHT theme.

Two families:

- Light-surface components whose text stayed on the dark palette. Fixed by
  completing the shell override, the method v6-light already used. But
  v6-light's own block was INCOMPLETE — 15 tokens against v9's 19 — so --hull,
  --cyan, --wire and --ok stayed dark inside the shell and .suggestion-chip
  (background var(--hull), color var(--text)) sat at 1.22:1, dark on dark. A
  partial fix of this family is more misleading than no fix at all: it makes
  the family look handled.

- Permanently dark panels (#references-panel, #archives-panel) whose text
  followed the theme. Given a panel-local dark palette, like the shell. Except
  #archives-panel is forced WHITE in v6-light, so that one theme out of seven
  needs a light exception — the blanket dark palette had put its three
  .btn-ghost at 2.95:1.

Also fixes the bench, which was accusing a compliant component. A gradient
background is not in `backgroundColor`, so .avatar (color #fff on
linear-gradient(135deg,#534ab7,#7f77dd)) was reported at 1.00:1 "white on
white" when it actually measures 6.93:1 on the dark stop and 3.76:1 on the
light one. Darkening its text would have DEGRADED a sound component. Gradient
backgrounds are now amplitude-tested: near-flat decorative veils (the body
tint, the shell gradient) remain measurable as a solid colour, while strong
ramps are declared NOT MEASURABLE and listed explicitly rather than silently
dropped. Two faults in that guard, both found by reading the output rather than
the code: Chromium serialises `transparent` as rgba(0,0,0,0), a zero-luminance
stop, so the body's 5%-tint veil read as a black-to-blue ramp and swallowed the
whole document; and reusing the composite-background walker was wrong, since it
only keeps nodes whose backgroundColor has alpha > 0 — exactly what a gradient
background is not. The gradient scan now has its own walk.

The coverage line now prints in the favourable case too. "Silence means clean"
is indistinguishable from "the bench looked at nothing", which is the ambiguity
behind every false zero in this bench.
