Correct four defects in my own contrast audit, and record the v131 findings

The v131 fixes themselves were committed in 83b68ea and 955a556. This commit is
about the instrument that measured them, the memory notes, and two skills.

The audit reported "0 elements below 3:1" where there was a defect. I only found
out by confronting it with a second bench. Four defects, all of which pushed the
count DOWN:

1. The background was not composited. `fondOpaque()` stopped at the first
   ancestor with alpha > 0.5 and returned it AS IS, so rgba(255,255,255,0.6) --
   a mid grey over a dark shell, measured rgb(155,154,158) -- was treated as
   white. Contrast was over-estimated and every translucent surface passed the
   threshold: the bench was blind to the --glass-bg bug it existed to measure.
   Fixed by compositing the whole translucent chain up to the first layer with
   alpha >= 0.999. This is what finally revealed the four defects in v6-cyber
   and v6-glass.

2. Node constants do not cross into page.evaluate. `CONTENEURS is not defined`
   crashed the bench, and the crash looked like a clean theme because nothing
   was printed. I nearly concluded "0 everywhere". Constants are now passed as
   arguments.

3. The guard against glyph-less containers was defeated by its own join:
   map(trim).join(" ") of empty nodes yields "    ", which is truthy. Seventeen
   containers with no glyph at all passed the filter (light read 56 instead of
   39). A trailing .trim() fixes it; --conteneurs brings them back for
   diagnosis.

4. --limite truncated the printout at 30 while the announced total said 56, so
   a partial list looked complete. Now overridable, with an explicit "N more
   not shown" line.

An audit also only measures what is RENDERED, and that is worth stating: the
bench now prints its COVERAGE. #memory-panel is display:none until it carries
.active and is invisible to the measurement; #references-panel is hidden by
translateX + opacity:0 and IS measured. The count is a LOWER BOUND, never a
certificate. Full-screen overlays are no longer opened by default: they cover
the side panels and change the composited background under them, so one audit
covers one group of panels.
