Fix the twelve theme tokens that were referenced but never defined

A `var(--x)` with no fallback makes the whole declaration invalid at
computed-value time: the property silently falls back to its initial value.
Twelve tokens were in that state, so every declaration using them had no
effect at all.

Found only after fixing the instrument. The old sweep read references with
`getPropertyValue(rule.style[i])`, which is blind to any var() carried by a
SHORTHAND: the CSSOM decomposes `background` into its longhands and returns ""
for every one of them, destroying the reference. Measured on `.badge-plasma`,
whose `background: var(--plasma-dim)` and `color: var(--plasma)` sit in the
same rule — only the second was seen, so the bench reported three missing
tokens when there were five. Reading `rule.cssText` keeps the shorthand intact.

The opposite trap came next: scanning the raw file surfaced var() citations
inside comments (the real use was `var(--app-h, 100vh)`, with a fallback) and
inside JS template strings. References now come from live rules only, and
tokens supplied at runtime through setProperty are excluded as out of scope.

Repaired: --gold, --plasma, --plasma-dim, --violet-dim, --text-muted, --bg,
--bg-dark, --bg-panel (all seven themes), --void, --grid, --plate, --neon-dim
(the three v6 themes) and --text-code (midnight).

Shared tokens are DERIVED from the active theme's own tokens, so they stay
correct in all seven themes without being repeated seven times. --gold,
--plasma and --violet must differ between light and dark, so they are
overridden in the five dark themes. --violet is new: .badge-violet and
.orchestrator-badge hardcoded #a78bfa, which measures 2.72:1 on a light
background.

Measured with contraste_jetons.js, which probes the real classes on a
var(--bg-card) surface and composites the translucent background chain up to
the first opaque one. Ten elements were below 3:1 before, six after; zero
regressions, four repairs. The remaining six are all in the light theme and
come from the v9 palette overriding it — handled next.

Benches: presets 48/48, smoothness 29/29, figures 27/27, annotations 17/17.
