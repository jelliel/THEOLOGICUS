The two captures committed in 955a556 as visual proof of the v6-cyber memory
panel fix were BYTE-IDENTICAL. Same sha1, 3924 bytes, a flat rectangle. They
proved nothing, and I presented them as evidence.

Three separate reasons, each of which alone was enough to ruin the capture:

1. apercu_refpanel.js hardcoded the capture target. Line 117 read
   `page.$("#references-panel")` regardless of --panneau, so asking for
   --panneau memory-panel measured the memory panel but photographed the
   references panel. Fixed: the capture follows the requested panel, and says
   so when the panel is missing.

2. The application was still LOCKED. Neither apercu_refpanel.js nor the audit
   seeded `theologicus_remember`, so #auth-overlay covered the page and the
   captures came out blank white. Fixed by reproducing the token exactly as the
   app builds it: sha256hex('remember:' + AUTH_HASH) wrapped in
   { v:1, mode:'admin', exp, tok }, read from writeRemember() in THEOLOGICUS.html.
   The setup wizard also has to be dismissed: theologicus_wizard_skipped='1' is
   not enough, the wizard shows whenever no AI identity is configured.

3. Element and clipped-region screenshots are unreliable on a panel that is
   translated and animated. `elementHandle.screenshot()` on the translucent
   panel returned a 1.3 kB uniform rectangle; a clipped page region landed
   beside the panel. capture_memoire.js now takes the WHOLE viewport, which
   cannot miss its target.

The panel is at [964,605,300,165] with opacity 1 at capture time in both runs.
What changes is the panel background: rgba(255,255,255,0.6) -- the white
--glass-bg inherited from :root -- versus rgba(18,12,28,0.62) after the fix.
