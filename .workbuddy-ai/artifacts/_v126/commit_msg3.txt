Confirm the spend before launching a paid video generation

The six AI providers bill per second of produced video, and the amount is
only known afterwards, on the provider's invoice. Nothing stopped a
long generation from being launched by a single click.

studioLaunch() now halts before sending when the selected source is in
the "ia" group -- the group is read from the service catalogue, so a
provider added upstream is covered without touching this code. The
confirmation shows the provider, and the volume to be billed as
videos x clips x seconds-per-clip = billed seconds.

No price is displayed: neither the app nor the service knows the
provider's rate at send time, so any figure would be invented. The
calculation is shown instead, so it can be checked, and the text says the
exact amount is only known afterwards.

The confirmation appears only for paid sources. Asking for it on Pexels
too would turn it into a reflex, and a confirmation read by reflex
protects nothing on the day it matters.

The bench intercepts /mpt/submit and /mpt/poll before clicking launch:
without that, every run would start a real generation and be billed for
it, and a test that costs money ends up removed. 121/121, no regression
(v123 78/78, v122 71/71, v125 56/56, v120 57/57 + 14/14, v124 18/18).
