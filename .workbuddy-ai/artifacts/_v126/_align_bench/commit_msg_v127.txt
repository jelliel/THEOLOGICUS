Fill the inside of figure shapes with a color, and rotate the arrows

The "Blocs Fleches" palette could only place a shape in outline; the user
asked to be able to rotate the arrows and to fill their interior with
colors.

Fill
- applyFigPaint() rewrites the generated SVG so that every element carries
  an explicit fill attribute: the requested color on closed shapes, "none"
  everywhere else.
- Only CLOSED shapes are filled (polygon, rect, circle, ellipse, or a path
  whose d ends with Z). An open path is left in outline on purpose: the
  browser implicitly closes it when painting, so filling a curved arrow
  would draw a blob instead of the arrow.
- For the BASIC shapes (Rectangle, Carre, Rond, Losange) the color is
  applied to the box background, which is how those shapes are drawn.
- A hatched "none" swatch and an empty-set button restore the outline.

Rotation
- applyFigRot() rotates the artwork (the <svg>), not the box, so the
  bounding box, the drag handles and the text stay upright.
- When the figure is rotated, preserveAspectRatio switches from "none" to
  "xMidYMid meet": otherwise a stretched arrow turned 90 degrees becomes
  short and fat, because the viewBox keeps being stretched along the
  original axes.
- Rotation is offered only for the arrow/path shapes; the four BASIC
  shapes are regular enough that rotating them adds nothing.

Controls
- The figure toolbar gains a fill color input, an empty-set button, a
  0-359 degree slider with its readout and a reset, and wraps onto a
  second row instead of overflowing, with its position clamped inside the
  container.
- The insertion menu gains a "Remplir" row (hatched none + the eight
  colors + a custom picker) and a "Rotation" row, both previewed live on
  the sample figure and applied to the next inserted figure.
- The export keeps the fill (background on the exported box); the
  rotation is not reported, since the export redraws a simplified box.
