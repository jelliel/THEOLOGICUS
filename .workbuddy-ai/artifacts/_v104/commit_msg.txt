v104 — stop the word card from covering the verse panel at its edges

The Hebrew word card (#hb-tip) could stay painted ON TOP of the verse
panel (#bible-verse-tip). Reported on a screenshot of Is 66:24 (921x838),
card of "tamut" (Strong 4191) sitting over the panel.

This was not a stacking bug: the card is at 9500, the panel at 9000, and
elementFromPoint confirmed the card wins. The defect was in __placerFiche:
the v101/v102 fallback — the one that caps the card so it leaves the
panel — was gated on `hote`, i.e. on a word STRICTLY contained in a
panel. A word at the very edge (panel padding, line overflow) makes
`hote` null, so the reduction was skipped and the card stayed on the
panel.

Fix: trigger the fallback as soon as `candidate.surPanneau`, and use
`hote` when available, otherwise the first panel the card actually
overlaps. Applies to the two scriptural panels and the four cards, which
share this helper.

Measured with a new bench (_v104/bench_overlap.js, 5/5): word mid-panel,
then panel pushed against the bottom of the window with the last verse's
word — 0 px overlap in both cases.

Non-regression: _v94 8/8, _v97 exhaustif 11/11 + garde_fiche 7/7 +
cas_dangereux 3/3, _v96 12/12, _v95 all OK, z-index guard static 0 /
browser 0, check_syntax 0 errors.

Bench trap worth recording: #theo-maj-bandeau (the "update available"
bar, z-index 99997) shows up on its own, covers the bottom strip and
swallows the mouse — a synthetic hover then misses its target and looks
like a stacking bug. It is absent from the user's screenshot.
